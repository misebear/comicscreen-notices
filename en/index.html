PageMoa is an Android comic and ebook reader for your own CBZ, ZIP, PDF, EPUB and TXT files. Find books by their covers and continue reading from Home.

Get PageMoa on Google Play

Bring your own library

Choose a book or connect a folder on your phone or tablet. PageMoa is a file reader, not a bookstore or a source of downloadable books. Android 10 or later is required; check device compatibility on Google Play.

More reading, less searching

Use the cover library, bookmarks and saved reading position. Adjust text size for text books and customize the page indicator's size, opacity or visibility.

Large local comic archives

Supported local ZIP and CBZ files can use direct reading without an additional full-file copy. Page caches still take space, and compatibility depends on the archive and document provider. The current file-size limit is 2 GiB. Opening a new book for the first time can take longer because the app checks the file.

Know the limits

74 interface language choices are available; untranslated strings may fall back to English. Automatic reading-progress sync across devices and additional native cloud sign-ins are not advertised as ready. Server-specific OPDS and NAS compatibility varies.
